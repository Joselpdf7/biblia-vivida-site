1. Who we are

The Vivid Bible app (the “app”) is developed and published by Fabricio Silva, an individual developer based in Brazil (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy under Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (LGPD) and other applicable laws.

2. Summary

  • The app has no sign-up or account. You can read, listen, save and take notes without giving a name or email.
  • What you do in the app is stored on your device.
  • Payments are processed by Apple. We never receive your card number.
  • The app does not use analytics tools, third-party crash reporting or advertising.
  • We do not sell personal data.

3. Data that stays on your device

Reading progress, saved cards, collections, history, journal, achievements, preferences (language, font size, reminder time) and passages recorded with the scanner are stored only on your iPhone. This information is not sent to us.

4. Subscription and purchases

The Pro subscription is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and only tells the app the purchase status. We use RevenueCat to validate subscriptions: it receives an anonymous identifier generated by the app and the transaction data provided by Apple (product, date, expiry, store country). No name, email or card data is shared with it. Legal basis: performance of a contract.

5. Reminders

Reading reminders are scheduled on the device itself, with your permission. We do not send server notifications. You can turn them off at any time in iPhone Settings.

6. Camera and photos

The scanner uses the camera or photo library only when you tap to take or choose a picture. The image is used to record the Bible reference and is not sent to us or to third parties.

7. Analytics and advertising

The app does not use usage analytics, third-party crash reporting or advertising. If you allowed analytics sharing in iPhone Settings, Apple may provide developers with aggregated, anonymous usage and crash statistics, under Apple’s privacy policy.

8. Service providers

We only share data with the vendors needed to run the app:

  • Apple: app distribution, payments and subscriptions.
  • RevenueCat: subscription validation.

We may also share data when required by law or by order of a competent authority.

9. International transfers

Apple and RevenueCat may store purchase data outside Brazil, including in the United States, with the safeguards provided by law.

10. How long we keep data

App data stays on the device until you delete it or remove the app. Subscription records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as long as needed for the subscription and for legal obligations.

11. Your rights

You may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, deletion and information about sharing of your data. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have similar rights under local law. Because the app has no account, most information exists only on your device, and deleting the app removes it. For requests about subscription data, email support@vividbible.app. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

12. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

13. Security

Purchases use Apple’s infrastructure, and communication with RevenueCat is encrypted. App data is protected by the iPhone’s own security features.

14. Changes

We may update this policy, for example if new features are released. The date at the top shows the current version, and relevant changes will be announced in the app.

15. Contact

Controller and contact for data requests: Fabricio Silva. Email: support@vividbible.app.
